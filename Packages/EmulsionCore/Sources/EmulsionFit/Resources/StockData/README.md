# Stock data

Provenance manifests for numeric readings of the published characteristic curves, spectral
sensitivity curves and spectral dye density curves of three motion-picture stocks, one folder per
stock. Generated by `Tools/stock-digitizing/digitize.py` from `Tools/stock-digitizing/charts.json`;
do not edit by hand.

Each folder holds `manifest.json`: source (document, revision, URL, PDF SHA-256), `filmType`
(`cameraNegative` or `printFilm`), method, and per chart its CSV file name and that CSV's
`sha256`, conditions, `gridStep`, axis calibration with per-tick residuals, line width,
uncertainty, each curve's extent with its `interpolatedSpans` and `sharedSpans`, and notes.

**The traced CSVs (`characteristic.csv`, `spectral_sensitivity.csv`, `spectral_dye_density.csv`)
are not in this repository** (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, 12 Nov 2026) and must not be added here. They
are local reference data: with the public PDFs, re-trace them into the reference-data folder
(`EMULSION_REFERENCE_DATA`, else `~/Documents/Emulsion Research/datasheet-tracings`):

```bash
venv/bin/python -I Tools/stock-digitizing/digitize.py --pdf-dir <PDFs> --out ~/"Documents/Emulsion Research/datasheet-tracings"
```

The output is byte-identical to what the manifests pin. `swift test` then also runs the checks on
the curves; the loader rejects a CSV whose SHA-256 differs from its manifest. Where the folder is
missing only the manifest checks run.

The camera negatives' folders also hold `negative-fit.stock.json`: the engine's negative
characteristic curves and printing-density matrix fitted to the traced curves by
`emulsion-fit negative --fit-date 2026-11-12` (stock format 2), with provenance and residual statistics
measured against the traced curves (no samples). The bundled default stock is the 250D fit under a
neutral name (MOV-25); the apps don't load these files.

## Columns

The traced CSVs are plain CSV, one header row, rows sorted ascending; an empty cell means the curve is not drawn there
or could not be separated from another line (see the manifest notes).

| File | x column | Curve columns |
|---|---|---|
| `characteristic.csv` | `log_exposure_lux_s`: log10 exposure in lux-seconds, steps of 0.05 | `red`, `green`, `blue`: density |
| `spectral_sensitivity.csv` | `wavelength_nm`, steps of 5 nm | `yellow_forming_layer`, `magenta_forming_layer`, `cyan_forming_layer`: log10 sensitivity, sensitivity = 1 / exposure (erg/cm²) giving the stated density |
| `spectral_dye_density.csv` | `wavelength_nm`, steps of 5 nm | negatives: `yellow`, `magenta`, `cyan`, `midscale_neutral`, `minimum_density`; 2383: `yellow`, `magenta`, `cyan`, `visual_neutral`: diffuse spectral density |

| 500T | 3200 K tungsten, 1/50 s; ECN-2; "Densitometry: ECN-2" (see below) | 1/25 s; ECN-2; Status M; D = 0.2 above D-min | ECN-2; D-mins subtracted |
| 2383 | 1/500 s tungsten + Heat Absorbing Glass No. 2043 + Series 1700 filter; ECP-2D; Status A | tungsten 1/50 s; ECP-2D; D = 1.0 | normalized to a visual neutral of 1.0 for a xenon-arc viewing illuminant; "CP-2D" |

## Method

1. The chart images are extracted from the PDFs at their native pixels (`pdfimages`; version in each manifest).
2. Each axis is calibrated by a linear fit over all its labelled ticks or grid lines, located to sub-pixel.
3. A line follower takes the centre of each drawn line across its local direction, through configured waypoints that guard against swapping curves at crossings.
4. Hidden stretches (crossings, grid lines, labels, dash gaps, curves lying on the axis) are interpolated or read from the stroke's visible edge, and every such stretch is listed in the manifest.
5. The centreline is resampled to the fixed grid inside each curve's drawn extent only; nothing is extrapolated or fitted.

## Uncertainty (1σ per point: reading error only)

| Stock | Chart | σx | σy | σy on the steepest 5 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 250D | characteristic | 0.015 log E | 0.009 D | 0.012 D |
| 250D | spectral sensitivity | 1.5 nm | 0.014 | 0.19 |
| 250D | dye density | 0.84 nm | 0.005 D | 0.015 D |
| 500T | characteristic | 0.064 log E (includes the uneven ticks) | 0.009 D | 0.036 D |
| 500T | spectral sensitivity | 1.05 nm | 0.013 | 0.12 |
| 500T | dye density | 1.0 nm | 0.005 D | 0.019 D |
| 2383 | characteristic | 0.015 log E | 0.018 D | 0.074 D |
| 2383 | spectral sensitivity | 1.7 nm | 0.015 | 0.26 |
| 2383 | dye density | 1.4 nm | 0.004 D | 0.022 D |

σ = (half the drawn line width + the axis's largest tick-fit residual) / pixels per unit. On steep
flanks the y error is dominated by the x error. Interpolated and shared stretches can be worse.

## Cross-check

An independent second tracing (600-dpi page renders, its own calibration and line follower) is
kept locally next to the main one (its notes: `Tools/stock-digitizing/crosscheck/tracing-b/report-b.md`);
`Tools/stock-digitizing/compare_tracings.py` writes `Tools/stock-digitizing/crosscheck/agreement.md`.
Measured on the local tracings, all characteristic curves agree within
0.003 D (negatives) and 0.019 D (2383) at the points traced here, and within 0.013 log E; spectral
curves agree to a few hundredths except on steep flanks and at the 2383 peaks.

## Things a user must know

1. These are tracings of published charts, not measurements of film. The stated uncertainty is
   reading error only; it does not cover how the publisher measured or drew the curves.
2. Negative dye curves (250D, 500T) are peak-normalized and D-min-subtracted, while Midscale
   Neutral and Minimum Density are not subtracted: compare the dye sum with neutral minus minimum
   density. The magenta curve is slightly negative near 445 nm (masking coupler).
3. The 2383 dye curves are plotted with peaks of about 0.81 / 0.88 / 1.09 (normalized to a visual
   neutral of 1.0), not peak-normalized, although the page text calls them peak-normalized.
4. 500T log-exposure axis: its ticks are unevenly drawn (about 0.06 log E systematic, in σx); its
   camera-stop ticks imply 0.3125 log E per stop, about 4 % more than log10(2), so slopes read on
   the log axis carry about 4 % ambiguity; and the chart says "Densitometry: ECN-2" (taken as Status M).
5. 250D: the camera-stop ticks agree with the log axis (0.300 log E per stop; stop 0 = −1.30 log lux·s).
6. 2383 spectral sensitivity: two unlabelled steep strokes at about 373–400 nm are not digitized
   because their layer cannot be identified; the magenta- and cyan-forming columns start at their
   labelled branches.
7. Licensing: the datasheets are © Eastman Kodak Company and are not redistributed here. The
   numbers are our own readings of the published curves; until the MOV-94 review clears, only these
   manifests are published. See `NOTICE` and `docs/STOCK-DATA.md`.
   Product names identify the source documents only.
